LOAN OR KEYS?

Kill the student loans first, or buy the house first? Everybody has an opinion. Almost nobody runs both orders, month by month, and compares the wealth at the end. This does.

Presets

Your Numbers

Method: both paths pay the loan minimum every month and live the same life. Keys First sends every spare dollar to the house fund, buys the moment the (appreciating) target is reachable, then attacks the loan. Loans First throws the spare cash at the loan, and once it dies the freed minimum joins the house fund. Wealth at the horizon = home equity + invested leftover cash − any loan still standing.

The Honest Caveats

This ignores taxes, tax and insurance inside the mortgage payment, PMI (which punishes small down payments — a real point for Loans First), employer retirement matches, and the fact that a house you cannot afford the repairs on is not wealth. It also ignores peace of mind: a paid-off loan is worth something no spreadsheet prices. If the gap is under $7,500 over a decade, take the order you will actually stick to.
